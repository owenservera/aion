## What this table does NOT establish

- No measured speed, satisfaction, consistency, model quality, export fidelity or user preference.
- No exhaustive comparison of the latest account rollouts, hidden flags, every model or mobile/Desktop parity.
- No proof that any UX affordance is implemented *by* the foundation model.
- No evidence of causal explanation beyond research hypotheses.
- No claim that the applications are equivalent in tool permissions, credit cost, model access or privacy.

## First practical comparison

Same user goal: `Show me the relationships among these 12 repositories, their agents, tools and cross-repo dependencies; produce an editable visual, evidence links and a way to revise an incorrect edge.`

Record the **actual visible user experience**, not just textual answer correctness:
- What control did the user invoke?
- What context/data was truly available?
- What native components appeared?
- Could the user inspect, adjust and export the map?
- What changed after an edit?
- How much manual work or navigation was required?
- Could the same task be resumed on Android?
