## Evidence labeling

- `VENDOR_DOCUMENTED`: a capability is stated by the vendor, not independently tested.
- `USER_OBSERVED`: owner supplied exact environment and outcome, not yet cross-checked.
- `RESEARCHER_OBSERVED`: direct dated UI experience, with limits and captured evidence.
- `COMPARATIVELY_TESTED`: matched tasks + recorded confounders and repeatability.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: expected difference, not a confirmed feature or causal fact.
- `UNKNOWN`: no credible accessible evidence.

When product documentation conflicts with real user observation, retain both and investigate plan/rollout/device/feature-flag differences rather than silently selecting a winner. Record source access date whenever refreshed.

## What to research next

Versioned product surfaces; app-shaped outputs and interactive controls; provenance relationships; mobile/desktop asymmetry; memory and cross-project boundaries; direct manipulate-vs-prompt experiences; text vs artifact loss across copy/export; usability and portability across personal workflows.
