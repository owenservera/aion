# evidence assessment


**Trigger:** paper, scientific claim, benchmark, contested result or high-impact tool choice. Write 1-sentence falsifiable claim; identify primary publication/DOI/version, study design, baselines, sample size, statistical uncertainty and independent validation where meaningful. Compare at least one plausible alternative or negative result; investigate retractions/corrections and publisher conflicts when material. Rank confidence HIGH (direct robust evidence/replicated), MEDIUM (credible but limited), LOW (preliminary/vendor-only), UNKNOWN (unverified/inaccessible) with explicit rationale. Separate `SOURCE_CLAIM`, `OBSERVATION`, `INFERENCE`, `TESTABLE_PROPOSAL`. Calculate only measured benefits; no fabricated percentages. Publish an evaluation recipe, applicability and cost of validation. Never present speculative science as established consensus.

**Deterministic companion:** Before routing high-impact conclusions, use [research-evidence-check](../research-evidence-check/SKILL.md) and its Node helper on a minimal **metadata-only** packet when practical. A passing schema check only proves packet structure/receipt consistency, not truth of the upstream claim.
