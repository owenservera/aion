# media intelligence


**Trigger:** YouTube, podcast, conference recording, article, blog or creator link. Determine actual content available: full audio/transcript, accessible caption, notes, abstract or only metadata. Record title, author/channel, episode date, URL, transcript provenance, language, timecodes when genuinely available and major factual claims. Isolate marketing/speculation from demonstrations; cross-check 1–2 consequential claims against independent authoritative sources. Extract short summary, novel ideas, contradictory evidence, 1–3 testable research questions and project fit. Respect copyright and platform access. For no transcript, never fabricate timestamps or claims; request no extra work unless the user truly needs full-audio analysis.
