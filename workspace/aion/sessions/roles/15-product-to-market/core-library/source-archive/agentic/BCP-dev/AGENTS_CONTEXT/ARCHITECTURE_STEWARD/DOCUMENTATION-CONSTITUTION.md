# Documentation Constitution

## D-01 — Canonical views over document sprawl

A new artifact may be local. A new permanent architectural concept must be represented in the canonical model.

## D-02 — Preserve source, normalize view

Never destroy useful source material merely because it is structurally non-conforming.

## D-03 — No parallel semantic hierarchies

A local workstream may introduce terminology for its work, but a durable cross-repository concept must map to the canonical vocabulary or explicitly record why it remains local.

## D-04 — Authority is referenced, not created

Documentation cannot promote itself into law.

## D-05 — Claims require lineage

A meaningful architectural claim should point to evidence, decision, source artifact, or explicit proposal status.

## D-06 — Ambiguity is data

Unknown, unresolved, contradictory and under-modelled states must remain visible.

## D-07 — Depth is explicit

Every substantive canonical representation has a defined architectural documentation depth.

## D-08 — Dependency is first-class

Important work is not considered structurally integrated until its relevant dependency edges are represented.

## D-09 — Views are rebuildable

Human-readable diagrams and summaries should be reproducible from canonical registries and source evidence where practical.

## D-10 — Drift is repairable

When source state changes, stale derived views must be detectable and repullable.

## D-11 — Historical material remains historical

Archiving is not deletion and historical evidence must not be silently reclassified as current authority.

## D-12 — Local quality does not determine global quality

A beautifully written subsystem document can still be structurally misplaced. The Steward judges integration, not literary quality.

## D-13 — Do not flatten

When a source contains a finer distinction than the current canonical model, preserve that distinction and decide whether a new node/edge/depth is needed.

## D-14 — No false completion

A mapped artifact is not necessarily proven, implemented or productized.

## D-15 — Every structural change has an impact surface

When a canonical responsibility, dependency or boundary changes, affected views and known dependents must be rechecked.
